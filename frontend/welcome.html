Why Tandurust

Tracking works. Typing every food is why people stop.

Most trackers make you search, pick and size each food separately. A home-cooked lunch turns into six entries, and after a few days most of us give up. Tandurust is for people who want to track and stay consistent without the chore.

The usual way

  1. Search "roti", scroll past 40 results, pick one
  2. Choose a serving size, change it to 2
  3. Search "dal", guess which of the dals it was
  4. Search "rice", raw or cooked, which bowl size
  5. Repeat at breakfast, snacks and dinner

About a dozen taps for one lunch

With Tandurust

"2 rotis, 100 g dal and 150 g cooked rice for lunch"

MacBro splits the meal into foods and works out calories, protein, carbs, fat and fiber. Foods you've logged before come straight from your own saved numbers.

How you log

Chat when you're busy. Add it yourself when you like.

The chat is the fastest way to stay consistent on busy days. Everything you'd expect from a traditional tracker is there too, and both ways fill the same day.

Chat with MacBro

For busy days and whole meals

  • One sentence for a whole meal, typed or spoken with the mic.
  • A card to check before anything is saved: Looks good, Needs changes or Cancel.
  • Past days too: pick a date and fill in a day you missed.
  • Packaged food? Scan its barcode and pick your pack: the real label, not a guess.

Add and edit by hand

For when you'd rather do it yourself

  • + Add food under any meal: type the food, amount and unit. Your saved foods pop up as you type and are added instantly.
  • Change an amount with the pencil on any food; calories and protein update as you type.
  • Move or copy a food to another meal, or delete it, in one tap.

Packaged food

Scan the barcode. Get the real label.

For anything in a packet, the numbers should come from its label, not a guess. Scan the barcode and Tandurust finds the product on Open Food Facts, a free, open database of food labels.

  • Camera or photoPoint your phone at the barcode and it's read on its own, or upload a photo of it. Photo too far away? It asks for a close-up of just the barcode.
  • You pick the pack"Is this your pack?" Tap yes, or see the next products. Nothing is assumed.
  • Or just name the brand"2 slices Modern multigrain bread": MacBro's card looks up the label for you, and uses his estimate only when there's no match.
  • Saved with label ✓Next time it's instant, with the label's exact numbers.

Every food you confirm is saved to My Foods. The next time you eat it, Tandurust uses exactly the same numbers, instantly, without asking the AI again. The foods you eat most become the fastest to log.

How it works

Three steps, and you're in control

Nothing is saved until you say so.

  1. Tell MacBro what you ate

    Say the food and how much. Plain words are fine, including Hindi food names and small typos.

  2. Check his card

    Calories and protein first, then each food and amount. Fix anything in plain words: "the rice was 200 g".

  3. Tap Looks good

    Your day updates straight away: calories left, protein, water and your streaks.

Also inside

Everything around your meals

Targets set for you

Calories, protein, carbs, fat and fiber worked out from your profile and goal. Adjust any number.

Progress and streaks

A meal-logging streak, a protein streak, and trends over 7, 14 or 30 days.

Water

A daily water goal with one-tap +250 ml and +500 ml, or just say "drank 1 L water".

Micronutrients

Iron, calcium, magnesium, potassium, zinc, vitamins C, B12 and D, and sodium, for every day.

Body Stats

Weight, BMI, an estimated body-fat percentage and body measurements over time.

Your data stays yours

Download everything or delete your account from Settings, any time.

Pricing

The AI part is free

Many trackers charge for AI logging. Tandurust is completely free to use today, AI included. Your saved foods, about 300 built-in foods, water and every button don't count towards the AI limit.

Why a limit at all? Tandurust runs on open-source AI models through free services, and those services cap how much we can use. So for now each account gets 20 AI messages a day, reset at midnight.

About Tandurust

Built in India, in 2026, for everyday eaters

तंदुरुस्तTandurust means "healthy, in good shape" in Hindi and Urdu.

Tandurust started in 2026 with a simple observation: people who track their food do better, especially with protein, and yet most of us stop after a few days. The problem was never motivation. It was typing every food, one by one, in apps that barely know a katori of dal from a bowl of pasta.

So we built a tracker you can simply talk to, that understands Indian home food, and that remembers what you eat. And because AI logging usually sits behind a subscription, we made it free to use, running on open-source AI models.

Our goal is a community of people who eat well and track consistently: students, working professionals, people training for strength or recomposition, and anyone who wants to understand their food without it taking over their day.
